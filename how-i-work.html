How I Work

Five habits you'll see on every project — and can check against the case studies.

  1. 1

    Write it down before building.

    Each project starts as a written document: what the problem is, the options, the decision, and any reversals. We agree on the plan first, then build. No drifting.

  2. 2

    Compare options, then customize.

    Instead of buying the first big platform, I compare the realistic choices against a written list of your needs — then adapt the best one with the smallest change possible. You get what fits, not what a sales team pushes.

  3. 3

    Keep the failures on record.

    When something doesn't work, it stays documented next to the working version — so nobody repeats the same costly mistake. One preserved failed app sits right beside the successful rebuild.

  4. 4

    Your systems, your data.

    Everything runs on infrastructure you own, with permissive licenses and backups before changes. If a vendor locks the door one day — as happened on a client project — you keep working.

  5. 5

    AI as my assistant, never my replacement.

    AI helps me research, build, document, and check my work — but requirements, verdicts, and review stay human. This very site is built so your AI can ask it questions too.
