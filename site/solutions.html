Solutions

Eight ways I help a business own its technology — from the database it runs on to the AI that answers its customers. No lock-in, no surprise bills.

Information Management Platform

A single, clean platform for your contacts, records and decisions — replacing scattered spreadsheets with a system your whole business can trust, with the right permissions for every role.

Multi-Channel Communication Facilitation

One shared inbox for WhatsApp, Messenger, social channels and more — so no conversation is lost, every reply is trackable, and your team works from a single view.

Social Media Integration

Official API connections — Meta, WhatsApp, TikTok and others — wired directly into your own systems, including custom app approvals and messaging pipelines.

Information Security

Row-level access control, private networking, air-gapped and self-hosted options, backups and recovery plans — your data stays yours and off the public internet.

AI Integration

Practical AI: RAG assistants grounded on your documents, chatbots that answer from your own knowledge, transcription and translation — no vendor lock-in.

Workflow Efficiency

Repetitive work automated with n8n, cron jobs and webhooks — incoming messages become records, reports build themselves, and your team stops copy-pasting.

Platform Integrations

Connecting the tools you already use — CRM, inbox, payments, analytics — so information flows automatically between them instead of through a person.

Consultation

An honest review of what you have, what to pick, and what to skip — a written recommendation before any build, so you never buy a platform you don't need.
